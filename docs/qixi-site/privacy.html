隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 2 日

本政策适用于“棋析”iOS / Android 移动应用及其支持页面。文中的“我们”指棋析的开发与维护方。隐私联系邮箱：xiangqistudio@outlook.com。

1. 本地数据

导入的题库、题目、答案树、练习记录、累计用时和设置默认保存在设备本地，用于提供残局训练、进度展示和界面设置。应用不会自动上传完整 CBL 文件。系统备份可能包含应用数据，具体由你的设备和备份设置决定。

2. 账号、学习与手动同步

本地训练不要求登录。你选择创建或登录账号时，我们处理你提交的登录名或邮箱、显示名称、密码哈希，以及为提供身份认证、机构角色、学习专题、作业和练习进度同步所必需的数据。密码不会以明文保存。

个人录谱、目录、注释和分析摘要仅在你点击同步时上传并与当前账号关联；登录本身不会自动上传这些内容。平台题库的学习进度、专题和作业会随登录账号同步。请勿在棋谱注释或其他同步内容中填写不必要的敏感信息。

学生账号由老师在班级管理中创建并设置初始密码。服务保存账号、机构及班级关系，作业接收关系和已提交的答题着法、错误与提示次数、分数、用时及提交时间，也保存平台练习进度、收藏、错题和学习日历。老师只能查看其有权管理的作业与学生记录。本机未确认提交的作业答案不会作为已交答案展示给老师。

3. 云库与可选 AI 服务

“云库对练”会向 ChessDB 发送当前棋盘局面（FEN）以获取应手。FEN 描述棋子位置、行棋方等局面信息，不包含你的姓名、邮箱或完整题库。接收请求的服务也会接触到 IP 地址、请求时间等网络连接信息；第三方如何保留和处理这些信息由其自身规则决定。

当云库没有可用应手，且你在设置中填写了 AI 服务地址时，棋析会向该地址申请访客凭证，并发送当前 FEN 和分析参数。AI 服务地址默认留空。第三方或你自行配置的服务由相应运营方处理请求，其数据保存方式由该服务决定；我们无法替这些服务承诺保存期限。你可以清空 AI 服务地址以停止后续 AI 请求；使用非云库训练模式可避免训练过程中的上述联网请求。

4. 客服、日志与支持网站

当你主动联系我们时，我们会收到你的邮件地址、邮件内容及附件，仅用于回复问题、排查故障和处理隐私请求。邮件通过 Outlook 邮箱处理，邮件服务商也会按其规则处理邮件。我们仅在处理相关事项所需期间保存这些信息；事项结束后，你可以请求删除，依法必须保存的信息除外。

为保障服务安全和排查故障，线上服务可能记录请求时间、IP 地址、请求路径、响应状态和必要的错误信息。支持页不设置广告、统计脚本或表单，页面由腾讯云服务器提供；腾讯云及网络服务商会依其规则处理基础网络连接信息。外部链接及邮箱服务受相应提供方的隐私规则约束。

5. 广告、权限与删除请求

当前移动版未集成广告或第三方行为分析 SDK，不使用广告标识符进行跨应用跟踪，不出售个人信息。导入文件由你在系统文件选择器中主动选择，应用不需要访问通讯录、麦克风或位置。

删除整本本地题库会删除该题库及其本地答题记录；仅移除一道残局会保留答题记录。删除应用可清除设备上的本地数据，系统备份和源文件需另行管理，卸载不会自动删除服务器数据。

登录后可在“我的 → 账号与机构 → 申请删除账号”输入当前密码并确认申请。平台管理员在申请后 7 日内人工处理账号及关联个人数据；受理申请不代表已经删除，处理前可在同一入口取消。删除范围包括全部机构关系、学习记录和云端个人棋谱；依法必须保留的信息按适用期限处理，系统备份按备份保留周期清除。访问、更正、客服邮件删除或无法登录时的请求可使用本页邮箱联系，并需核实身份。第三方服务保存的信息，请向相应服务提供方提出请求。

6. 未成年人和政策更新

学生学习服务可能由未成年人使用。老师及机构应在取得所需监护人同意后创建学生账号，尽量使用昵称，不填写无关个人信息。监护人可协助查看、更正和申请删除学生账号与学习记录；请通过本页邮箱联系并核实关系。未成年人联系客户支持时，请由监护人协助。

当功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策及页面日期；涉及需要告知或征得同意的变化时，会提供相应提示。

Privacy Policy — Qixi

Updated October 2, 2026

This policy covers the Qixi iOS / Android app and its support pages. “We” means the developer and maintainer of Qixi. Contact: xiangqistudio@outlook.com.

Local data

Imported libraries, puzzles, solution trees, practice records, elapsed time and preferences are stored on your device by default to provide training, progress and settings features. The app does not automatically upload complete CBL files. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings.

Account, learning and manual sync

Local training does not require sign-in. When you choose to create or sign in to an account, we process the login name or email, display name, password hash, and data necessary for authentication, organisation roles, learning topics, assignments and practice-progress sync. Passwords are not stored in plaintext.

Personal manuals, folders, notes and analysis summaries are associated with your account only when you start a sync; signing in alone does not upload them. Platform-library learning progress, topics and assignments sync with your signed-in account. Do not put unnecessary sensitive information in manual notes or other synced content.

Teachers create student accounts and initial passwords. The service stores account, organisation and class relationships, assigned homework, submitted moves, mistakes, hints, scores, elapsed time and submission dates, plus platform progress, favourites, wrong-answer records and learning history. Teachers can access only authorised assignments and students. Unconfirmed local homework drafts are not shown as submitted answers.

Network features

Cloud practice sends the current board position (FEN) to ChessDB to obtain a reply. FEN describes the board and side to move, not your name, email or complete library. The receiving service also receives network information such as your IP address and request time.

If no cloud reply is available and you configure an AI server, the app requests a guest credential and sends the current FEN and analysis parameters to that server. The AI address is empty by default. External services control their own processing and retention; we cannot promise retention periods on their behalf. Clear the AI address to stop future AI requests. Choose a non-cloud training mode to avoid these training network requests.

Support, logs and website

When you email us, we receive your address, message and attachments to answer questions, troubleshoot and handle privacy requests. Email is processed through Outlook. We retain this information only as needed for these purposes; you may request deletion after the matter is resolved, except where retention is legally required.

For service security and troubleshooting, online services may record request time, IP address, request path, response status and necessary error information. These support pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts or forms and are served from Tencent Cloud. Tencent Cloud and network providers process basic network connection information under their own rules. External services have their own privacy rules.

Advertising, permissions and requests

The current app has no advertising or third-party behavioural analytics SDKs, does not use advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking, and does not sell personal information. You select files through the system file picker; contacts, microphone and location access are not required. Delete an app library to remove its puzzles and practice records, or delete the app to remove local data. Manage original files and backups separately. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of account, synced or support information; we may verify a request with the original email address or account information. Contact external providers about data they retain.

Children and changes

To request deletion, sign in and open My → Account and organisation → Request account deletion, enter your current password and confirm. An administrator manually processes the account and associated personal data within seven days; acceptance is not deletion. Cancel a pending request at the same location. Deletion covers organisation relationships, learning records and personal cloud manuals. Legally required records and backups follow their applicable retention periods. Uninstalling does not delete server data. Email us if you cannot sign in.

Students may be minors. Teachers and organisations must obtain any required guardian consent before creating accounts and should use nicknames without unnecessary personal details. Guardians may help access, correct or request deletion of a student’s account and learning records; contact us to verify the relationship. Minors should contact support with a guardian’s help. We will update this policy and its date when practices change and provide notices or seek consent where required.
