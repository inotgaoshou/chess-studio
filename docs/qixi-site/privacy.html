隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 7 日

本政策适用于“棋析”iOS / Android 移动应用及其支持页面。文中的“我们”指棋析的开发与维护方。隐私联系邮箱：xiangqistudio@outlook.com。本政策不适用于 Xiangqi Studio 桌面版的账号或同步服务。

1. 本地数据

棋析移动版无需注册账号。导入的题库、题目、答案树、练习记录、累计用时和设置保存在设备本地，用于提供残局训练、进度展示和界面设置。应用不会自动上传完整 CBL 文件或答题记录。系统备份可能包含应用数据，具体由你的设备和备份设置决定。

2. 云库与可选 AI 服务

“云库对练”会向 ChessDB 发送当前棋盘局面（FEN）以获取应手。FEN 描述棋子位置、行棋方等局面信息，不包含你的姓名、邮箱或完整题库。接收请求的服务也会接触到 IP 地址、请求时间等网络连接信息；第三方如何保留和处理这些信息由其自身规则决定。

当云库没有可用应手，且你在设置中填写了 AI 服务地址时，棋析会向该地址申请访客凭证，并发送当前 FEN 和分析参数。AI 服务地址默认留空。第三方或你自行配置的服务由相应运营方处理请求，其数据保存方式由该服务决定；我们无法替这些服务承诺保存期限。你可以清空 AI 服务地址以停止后续 AI 请求；使用非云库训练模式可避免训练过程中的上述联网请求。

3. 客服邮件

当你主动联系我们时，我们会收到你的邮件地址、邮件内容及附件，仅用于回复问题、排查故障和处理隐私请求。邮件通过 Outlook 邮箱处理，邮件服务商也会按其规则处理邮件。我们仅在处理相关事项所需期间保存这些信息；事项结束后，你可以请求删除，依法必须保存的信息除外。请只提供解决问题所必需的信息。

4. 广告、权限与支持网站

当前移动版未集成广告或第三方行为分析 SDK，不使用广告标识符进行跨应用跟踪，不出售个人信息。导入文件由你在系统文件选择器中主动选择，应用不需要访问通讯录、麦克风或位置。

支持页不设置广告、统计脚本或表单。页面由 GitHub Pages 托管时，GitHub 会按照其 隐私声明 处理访问日志等信息。外部链接及邮箱服务受相应提供方的隐私规则约束。

5. 删除与个人信息请求

删除整本题库会删除该题库及其答题记录；仅移除一道残局会保留答题记录。删除应用可清除设备上的应用本地数据，系统备份和源文件需另行管理。iOS 的“卸载 App”会保留部分数据，请使用“删除 App”。我们没有可远程操作你的本地题库的接口。

对于你通过客服邮件提供的信息，可联系本页邮箱申请访问、更正或删除。我们可能需要通过原联系邮箱核实请求，以避免将信息提供给错误的人。针对第三方服务保存的信息，应向相应服务提供方提出请求。

6. 未成年人和政策更新

我们不会以收集儿童个人信息为目的提供服务。未成年人联系客户支持时，请由监护人协助，避免提供无关个人信息。若发现误提供的儿童个人信息，请联系本页邮箱处理。

当功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策及页面日期；涉及需要告知或征得同意的变化时，会提供相应提示。

Privacy Policy — Qixi

Updated September 7, 2026

This policy covers the Qixi iOS / Android app and its support pages, not the Xiangqi Studio desktop account or sync services. “We” means the developer and maintainer of Qixi. Contact: xiangqistudio@outlook.com.

Local data

No app registration is required. Imported libraries, puzzles, solutions, practice records, elapsed time and preferences are stored on your device to provide training and progress features. The app does not automatically upload complete CBL files or practice records. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings.

Network features

Cloud practice sends the current board position (FEN) to ChessDB to obtain a reply. FEN describes the board and side to move, not your name, email or complete library. The receiving service also receives network information such as your IP address and request time.

If no cloud reply is available and you configure an AI server, the app requests a guest credential and sends the current FEN and analysis parameters to that server. The AI address is empty by default. External services control their own processing and retention; we cannot promise retention periods on their behalf. Clear the AI address to stop future AI requests. Choose a non-cloud training mode to avoid these training network requests.

Support and website

When you email us, we receive your address, message and attachments to answer questions, troubleshoot and handle privacy requests. Email is processed through Outlook. We retain this information only as needed for these purposes; you may request deletion after the matter is resolved, except where retention is legally required.

The current app has no advertising or third-party behavioural analytics SDKs, does not use advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking, and does not sell personal information. You select files through the system file picker; contacts, microphone and location access are not required. These support pages have no advertising, analytics scripts or forms. When hosted on GitHub Pages, access information is processed under GitHub’s privacy statement. External services have their own privacy rules.

Deletion and requests

Deleting a library removes its practice records; removing an individual puzzle retains its records. Delete the app to remove its local data. On iOS, use Delete App rather than Offload App. Manage original files and backups separately. We cannot remotely access or delete local libraries. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support. We may verify requests through your original email address. Contact external providers about data they retain.

Children and changes

We do not intentionally seek personal information from children. Minors should contact support with a guardian’s help and avoid unnecessary personal information. Contact us if such information was sent by mistake. We will update this policy and its date when practices change and provide notices or seek consent where required.
