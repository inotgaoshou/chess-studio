2. App purpose and target audience

Qiyan is a Chinese chess endgame training app for Chinese chess learners, players, and coaches. It imports CBL endgame files, presents playable positions and solution branches, records practice progress locally, and provides several training modes. It helps users repeatedly study and solve endgame positions on an iPhone or iPad.

3. Access and setup instructions

No account, registration, login, or demo credentials are required.

a. Launch Qiyan.
b. Tap \"导入 CBL\" (Import CBL).
c. Extract the attached qiyan-review-sample-cbl.zip file, then select qiyan-review-sample.cbl from the iOS Files picker.
d. Select the imported library and choose a problem.
e. Choose a training mode and move a piece by tapping its source and destination squares.

4. External services, tools, and platforms

- ChessDB (https://www.chessdb.cn/) is used by Cloud Practice to obtain move suggestions. Only the current board position in FEN format is sent.
- Users may optionally enter their own AI service URL. This field is empty by default.
- Apple's Files picker and on-device SQLite storage are used for importing and storing libraries and practice records.
- The app uses the open-source Capacitor, React, and Lucide software libraries under their applicable licenses.

The app has no advertising, analytics SDK, authentication provider, payment processor, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or paid content.

5. Regional differences

The app provides the same features and content in every region where it is available. Cloud Practice requires network access to ChessDB. Imported libraries, solution practice, replay, and free-play functions remain available without that cloud request.
